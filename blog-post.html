5 tips for your first driving lesson

Feeling nervous is normal. Here is how to make the first hour easier.

1. Bring your licence

You need your provisional licence with you. Wear flat, comfortable shoes and bring glasses if you use them.

2. Tell your instructor how you feel

Instructors have taught hundreds of nervous learners. A quick chat helps them set the right pace.

3. Expect a quiet road

You will start in a car park or empty street, learning the controls before any traffic.

4. Ask questions

If something is unclear, ask. Good instructors want you to understand, not just copy.

5. Rest afterwards

Concentrating is tiring. Read your lesson notes later, when you feel fresh.
