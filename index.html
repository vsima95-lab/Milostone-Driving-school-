From first lesson to licence

Four steps, and we guide you through each one.

  1. Book an intro call

    Tell us your experience and goals. We match you with the right instructor and car.

  2. Learn the basics

    Quiet roads first. You build control before facing traffic.

  3. Practise real routes

    We drive the roads used on test day until they feel familiar.

  4. Take your test

    We collect you, warm you up and wait for you afterwards.

Common questions

How many lessons will I need?

It depends on your starting point. Most learners need 30 to 45 hours in total, including private practice.

Can I cancel or move a lesson?

Yes. Free changes up to 24 hours before. After that we charge half the lesson price.

Do you provide a car for the test?

Yes. It is included in the Test Ready package and costs £70 with other packages.

Which areas do you cover?

The whole city and surrounding suburbs. Ask us if you are not sure about your postcode.
