About Milestone

Local instructors, modern cars and lessons that fit your life.

A calmer way to learn

We started Milestone in 2008 after seeing too many people give up on driving because lessons felt stressful.

Today we teach more than 400 learners every year. Each lesson has a clear goal, a short recap and time to ask questions.

How we teach

Patience first

You set the pace. We never rush you into traffic you are not ready for.

Clear feedback

After every lesson you get short notes on what went well and what to practise.

Safety always

Dual-control cars, regularly serviced and fully insured.
